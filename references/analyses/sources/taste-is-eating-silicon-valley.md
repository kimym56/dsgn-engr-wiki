# Source analysis: Taste Is Eating Silicon Valley.

## Editorial evaluation

### Scope fit

Anu's 2024 essay argues that as software production becomes less scarce, product design, brand, experience, cultural awareness, and editorial judgment become stronger differentiators. That provocation is relevant to design engineering because it treats implementation quality as necessary but insufficient and asks how technical work can carry a coherent point of view. It is stronger as a framing essay than as operational guidance: its claims about market convergence and investment behavior are argued through examples, not systematic evidence.

### Authority and durability

The Working Theorys page attributes the essay to `Anu` and dates it September 19, 2024; page metadata also records a later modification. The author defines taste explicitly and acknowledges limits to the thesis, including domains where technical novelty remains primary. The essay is a first-person argument published on Substack, not a research report, and some examples and AI-market comparisons will age. The canonical URL was directly reachable without authentication.

### Overlap and classification

Proposed format: `article`. Proposed Areas: `design-engineering-foundations` and `product-craft`. Proposed Collections: none. Source language: `en`. It overlaps directly with `developing-taste` and `on-taste-part-3`: this source supplies the market-and-culture thesis, while the other two focus on training judgment.

### Editorial recommendation

`review`. Retain as a clearly labeled perspective piece. Its durable value is the distinction between functional adequacy and culturally resonant product expression; editors should not present its forecasts or named-company judgments as settled fact.

## Website design analysis

### Information hierarchy and navigation

The page follows a conventional newsletter-essay hierarchy: title, thesis-like subtitle, date and social actions, a sequence of emphatic section headings, numbered notes, related reading, discussion, and subscription chrome. Repeated declarative headings make the argument scannable, although platform actions and comments extend the page well beyond the article.

### UI, interaction, and motion

The delivered document exposes labeled search, share, reaction, comment, menu, and subscription controls. Heading-level link buttons support deep linking. Interactive states and motion timing could not be exercised because no browser runtime was available, so focus behavior, menu behavior, and reduced-motion support are unverified.

### Grid, layout, and responsive behavior

The source is organized as a single long-form reading column with full-width platform chrome around it. A responsive viewport is declared, but rendered breakpoint behavior was not available for inspection.

### Typography, color, and visual rhythm

Short, forceful headings interrupt longer prose blocks and quotations, creating an editorial cadence around the repeated word “taste.” Page metadata declares a warm off-white theme color. Exact typography, contrast, and dark-mode behavior were not visually verified.

### Accessibility observations

The delivered HTML includes an `article`, a main region labeled `Post`, labeled regions, explicit labels on many icon buttons, and hidden decorative icons. The subscription field uses `type="email"`. The viewport disables user scaling with `user-scalable=0`, which can interfere with zoom-based access. Keyboard order, visible focus, contrast, and live interaction behavior remain untested.

### Transferable principles

- Treat product quality as a combination of utility, interaction, brand, and cultural context.
- Make an editorial position explicit enough to guide tradeoffs.
- Separate a provocative thesis from the evidence needed to operationalize it.
- Use repeated section claims to keep a long argument navigable.

### Source-specific expression to avoid copying

Do not reuse the “eating Silicon Valley” framing, the essay's slogans, examples, warm publication identity, Substack composition, imagery, or recommendation-card treatment.
